Beginner rule: If you did not cook it simply, and it is sweet, fried, bakery, or packaged — skip it. When confused, eat eggs + sabzi + salad.

Vegetables — KEEP (eat freely)

These barely raise sugar. Fill half your plate.

  • Palak, methi leaves, lauki, tinda, tori, bhindi
  • Cabbage, cauliflower, broccoli, beans, cluster beans
  • Cucumber, tomato, onion, capsicum, lettuce, zucchini
  • Karela, bottle gourd, ridge gourd, mushroom
  • Salad before lunch and dinner every day

Vegetables — LIMIT or AVOID

Not “poison” — they act more like rice/roti for your sugar.

  • Avoid fried: aloo bhujia, fries, pakora, samosa stuffing
  • Limit potato / arbi / sweet potato — max 2–3 small pieces, 1–2×/week, never fried
  • Limit corn, beetroot, green peas — small side only
  • Avoid: canned veggies in sweet sauce, ketchup-heavy mixes
  • Avoid vegetable juice (even carrot/beet) — sugar without fibre

Black coffee — YES

  • Black coffee without sugar is OK — 1–2 cups/day
  • Can help alertness; may slightly support metabolism
  • No sugar, no honey, no flavoured syrup, no creamers
  • If empty stomach gives acidity — drink after breakfast
  • Stop after 4 pm if it hurts sleep

Milk, curd, dairy

  • Curd / plain dahi — KEEP best dairy 100–150 g unsweetened with lunch
  • Chaas / buttermilk — KEEP (no sugar, light salt OK)
  • Paneer — KEEP 80–100 g; grilled/bhurji, not deep fried
  • Milk — LIMIT ½–1 cup toned/double-toned if you need tea base; no full-cream litres; no sweet milkshakes
  • AVOID flavoured yogurt, sweet lassi, condensed milk, ice cream, cheese burst, cream gravies

KEEP eating (simple shopping list)

  • Eggs, chicken, fish, dal, chana, sprouts, paneer
  • Jowar / bajra / ragi flour · oats
  • All “KEEP” vegetables above · salad items
  • Plain curd · buttermilk · almonds (6–8) · flax/chia
  • Lemon, cinnamon, methi seeds, karela
  • Black coffee / green tea / lemon water — no sugar

STOP eating

  • Sugar, sweets, biscuits, bakery, bread, maida, Maggi
  • White rice, biryani, fried rice (14-day ban)
  • Soft drinks, packaged juice, sweet tea
  • Alcohol · deep fried · chips · namkeen
  • Banana, mango, grapes, chikoo, dried fruit
